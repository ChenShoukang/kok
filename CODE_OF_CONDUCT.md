# 社区约定 / Community conduct

欢迎不同经验与背景的参与者。讨论围绕具体行为、证据与代码；提出问题时给出复现步骤，反馈设计时说明使用场景。

- 尊重他人；不允许骚扰、歧视、人身攻击、威胁或泄露个人信息。
- 未经许可，不发布他人的密钥、私人文件、日志或截图。
- 分歧可以保留；避免重复刷屏、辱骂和无关推广。
- 维护者可移除违规内容、关闭讨论或限制参与。处理问题时应说明理由，并避免继续传播私人信息。

不含私人信息的问题可联系该讨论的维护者。涉及隐私或安全时使用[安全报告方式](SECURITY.md)，不要在公开 Issue 中复述敏感内容。

Participants of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome. Keep discussion focused on behavior, evidence and code. Provide reproduction steps and explain the use case behind design feedback. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing and unauthorized sharing of private data are not acceptable. Avoid spam and unrelated promotion. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or limit participation, explaining the reason without amplifying private information. Contact the discussion's maintainer for ordinary concerns; use [private security reporting](SECURITY.en.md) for sensitive matters.
